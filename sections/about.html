In brief

Dr. Emmanuel A. Owolabi is a physician, health innovator and institution builder committed to advancing healthcare in Africa through technology, research, education and leadership. He is the Founder of EOhealth, a health-innovation and education platform making reliable health information accessible, developing emerging healthcare professionals and building practical responses to healthcare challenges. Through the Virtual Health Literacy and Emergency Response Training, he has contributed to training more than 400 participants from over 30 medical schools. He also founded MentorMatrix Network, a mentorship and capacity-building platform helping young people gain practical guidance, recognise opportunities and build careers of impact. He is the Founder of Publish to Greatness, a multidisciplinary research mentorship community comprising five active groups and 54 members. His work and interests span artificial intelligence in medicine, digital health, medical education, antimicrobial resistance, research-capacity development and health-systems strengthening.
