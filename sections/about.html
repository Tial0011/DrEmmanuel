01 / ABOUT

Medicine gave me a profession. Building solutions gave the profession a wider purpose.

My work began with a simple desire to make reliable health information easier to understand. It has grown into a connected body of work spanning public health communication, mentorship, research capacity, digital health and institution building.

I believe Africa's healthcare future will be shaped not only by what we know, but by how well we develop people, translate evidence, deploy technology responsibly and build systems that endure.
