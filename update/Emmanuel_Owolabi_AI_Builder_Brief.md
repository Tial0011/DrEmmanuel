# Website Upgrade: Designer & AI Implementation Brief
**dremmanuelowolabi.com | Version 1.0 | 15 November 2026**
**Decision:** Improve the existing personal website; do not invent facts or turn it into a hospital booking site.

## 1. Outcome and audience
- Primary outcome: a visitor understands who Emmanuel is, sees independently verifiable work and can make a relevant enquiry within a few minutes.
- Audiences: (a) institutions/research collaborators, (b) health innovation partners, (c) speaking/media organisers. Give each a distinct route without three competing brands.
- Success criteria: clear first screen, credible proof, accessible navigation, working enquiry forms, excellent mobile experience. No invented numerical performance promises.

## 2. Verified live-site inventory — preserve and refine
- Current hero: “Building people, institutions and practical solutions for better healthcare in Africa.” Preserve this strong central idea.
- Current introduction identifies Dr. Emmanuel A. Owolabi as physician, health innovator and 2026 Diana Award recipient. Confirm exact preferred titles and award citation before publishing.
- Existing sections: About; Work & Impact (EOhealth, MentorMatrix Network, Publish to Greatness); Research; Recognition; Speaking & Media; Impact in Practice gallery; Insights; Contact. Existing biography pack, speaker sheet and media kit links should be retained and tested.
- Live site currently states 400+ people trained, 30+ medical schools, three founder-led platforms, five active Publish to Greatness groups and 54 members. All metrics must be checked for date, definition and evidence before reuse.
- Existing speaking themes: AI & Healthcare Innovation, Health Literacy & Medical Education, Research to Reality, Leadership & Institution Building. Existing insight themes: The African Health Fix, Research to Reality, Beyond the White Coat.
- Source reviewed: https://dremmanuelowolabi.com/ (15 Nov 2026). This was a content review, not a measured visual/mobile performance test.

## 3. Recommended navigation and page structure
- HOME: concise hero; credibility strip; three audience paths; 3 featured initiatives; selected research/impact; selected media; final contact CTA.
- ABOUT: professional story, current work, verified timeline, values, downloadable biography/CV. Avoid long self-congratulatory lists.
- WORK & IMPACT: EOhealth, MentorMatrix Network, Publish to Greatness and ViHLERT as a programme under its appropriate parent. Each has an evidence-backed case study, not just a logo tile.
- RESEARCH: research interests separated from actual outputs; list papers with DOI, role, coauthors and status; presentations with verifiable conference references. Do not call unpublished work published.
- SPEAKING & MEDIA: four focused topics; selected engagements; speaker bio; approved headshots; downloadable press kit; invitation form.
- INSIGHTS: retain three existing editorial lenses; publish only finished, dated articles with author, sources where relevant and working links.
- CONTACT / COLLABORATE: one page with selectable enquiry types: research/institutional, healthcare innovation, speaking/media, general. No patient data collection.

## 5. Project case-study template
- For each initiative include: (1) problem addressed, (2) who it serves, (3) solution delivered, (4) Emmanuel’s exact role and dates, (5) collaborators and credits, (6) evidence-backed outcomes with measurement dates, (7) links to project website and media, (8) current status and next steps.
- Distinguish organisation from programme: ViHLERT should not appear as a separate founded organisation if it is an EOhealth programme. Verify all relationships.
- Use authentic organisation marks only, supplied or independently verified. If unavailable, use typography instead of AI-generated logos.

## 6. Visual design specification
- Art direction: refined editorial, modern African professional, human, confident, calm; no flashy “startup dashboard” appearance.
- Palette proposal: deep navy #10283F, warm off-white #FAF9F6, charcoal #26323B, muted gold #B58B45. Test contrast before adoption; colour choices require owner approval.
- Typography: one elegant heading family and one highly legible body family; avoid more than two families and avoid excessively small text.
- Use authentic professional portraits and event photographs with permission and meaningful captions; no synthetic images of Emmanuel or fictional events.
- Mobile-first: readable hero, no overlapping portrait and headline, visible CTA, concise menu, responsive cards. Restrained motion only; honour reduced-motion settings.
- Maintain consistent spacing, image crops, link states, hover/focus treatment and clear visual hierarchy.

## 7. Functionality and engineering acceptance criteria
- Every button, document download, external project link, email link and form submission must be tested. Replace dead or placeholder links.
- Contact form: name, email, organisation (optional), enquiry type, short message, consent checkbox, confirmation state and anti-spam protection; send to owner-approved destination. Never request medical histories.
- Owner must be able to edit biographies, projects, research, metrics, media, photographs and article posts without asking developer to change code.
- SEO: unique page titles and descriptions, semantic headings, sitemap, canonical URLs, social sharing images, descriptive image alt text and accurate Person structured data where applicable.
- Performance: target Google “good” Core Web Vitals on mobile (LCP <=2.5 s, INP <=200 ms, CLS <=0.1) using field data where available; record Lighthouse lab results separately.
- Accessibility: target WCAG 2.2 AA; test keyboard, focus, contrast, screen readers, zoom, form errors and reduced motion.
- Privacy/security: HTTPS, secure form delivery, least-privilege admin, backups, privacy notice for analytics and enquiry processing, Nigeria Data Protection Act considerations; no unapproved trackers.
- Preserve existing URLs where possible; set 301 redirects for changed URLs. Test on real iOS Safari, Android Chrome and desktop browsers.
