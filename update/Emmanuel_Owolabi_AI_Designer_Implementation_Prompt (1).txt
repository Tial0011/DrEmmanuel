It must support three audiences—institutions and research collaborators; healthcare innovators and strategic partners; speaking and media organisers—through one coherent identity. Success test: within 10 seconds a first-time visitor understands who Emmanuel is, what he builds, why he is credible, and how to contact him for the relevant opportunity.

# 02  Brand system: premium, memorable, human

- Positioning: Physician • Healthcare Innovator • Researcher • Institution Builder. Core idea: “Building people, institutions and practical solutions for better healthcare in Africa.” Retain unless owner approves a change.

- Visual mood: editorial luxury meets contemporary health technology; calm authority, confident whitespace, cinematic but authentic photography, deliberate visual hierarchy. Not generic startup gradients, loud gold, stock-hospital imagery or AI-generated people.

# 03  Interface design specifications

- Desktop hero: generous white/ivory negative space; 55/45 editorial composition with headline and supporting copy left, original portrait right; subtle navy panel or image backdrop; two clear CTAs. On mobile stack text above portrait, preserve readable crop and visible CTA.

- Sticky but compact navigation: Home, About, Work & Impact, Research, Speaking & Media, Insights, Contact. Highlight “Work With Me” only if it routes to useful contact choices. Mobile menu must be keyboard accessible.

- Use restrained micro-interactions (150–250 ms), refined hover states, subtle section reveals only when motion preference permits. Never use auto-playing audio, scroll hijacking, excessive parallax, carousels that hide essential content, or decorative loading screens.

- Cards: large image or precise typographic hierarchy; 12–16 px corner radius at most; hairline borders or soft shadows; clear clickable affordances. Keep layouts airy, consistent and polished across breakpoints.

- Footer: compact biography, navigation, verified social links, professional email, copyright, privacy notice. Avoid broken or placeholder links.

# 06  Engineering and quality bar

- Mobile-first responsive layouts at ~360, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 px; no horizontal overflow, awkward hero crops, hidden buttons or tiny tap targets.

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA: semantic landmarks/headings, keyboard navigation, focus states, descriptive links, alt text, form labels/errors, contrast, reduced motion and screen-reader checks.

- Target excellent Core Web Vitals at 75th percentile: LCP ≤2.5s, INP ≤200ms, CLS ≤0.1; verify with field data when available. Optimise modern image formats, responsive sizes, font loading and JS.

- Technical SEO: unique titles/descriptions, canonical URLs, sitemap, robots rules, clean URLs, correct Open Graph/social cards, structured Person/Article data where accurate, and no indexation of staging.

- Security/privacy: HTTPS, form anti-spam, data minimisation, privacy notice, access control, backup and rollback plan. No exposed API keys, analytics without proper consent where required.

- Content maintenance: simple CMS or documented editing workflow for projects, metrics, articles, speaking engagements and publications; reusable components and clean handover notes.
